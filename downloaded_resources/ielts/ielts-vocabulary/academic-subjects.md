Here's a lesson on vocabulary related to "Academic Subjects" for the IELTS band score 3.0-4.5:

**I. Language and Literature Subjects:**

- _English Language_: The study of English grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, and speaking skills.

- _Literature_: The study of written works, including novels, plays, poems, and short stories.

- _Foreign Languages_: The study of languages other than one's native language, such as Spanish, French, or German.

- _Linguistics_: The scientific study of language, including its structure, sounds, and meaning.

- _Creative Writing_: The practice of writing stories, poems, or other forms of artistic expression.


**II.Mathematics and Sciences:**

- _Mathematics_: The study of numbers, quantities, structures, and relationships.

- _Algebra_: The branch of mathematics that deals with symbols, equations, and mathematical operations.

- _Geometry_: The study of shapes, sizes, and properties of figures and spaces.

- _Biology_: The study of living organisms, their structure, function, and interactions with the environment.

- _Chemistry_: The study of the composition, properties, and reactions of substances.


**III. Social Sciences:**

- _History_: The study of past events, people, and civilizations.

- _Geography_: The study of Earth's physical features, climate, and human populations.

- _Economics_: The study of production, consumption, and distribution of goods and services.

- _Psychology_: The study of the human mind, behavior, and mental processes.

- _Sociology_: The study of society, social relationships, and institutions.


**IV. Physical Education and Health:**

- _Physical Education (PE)_: The study of physical fitness, sports, and overall well-being.

- _Health Education_: The study of personal health, nutrition, and healthy lifestyle choices.

- _Anatomy_: The study of the structure and function of the human body.

- _Physiology_: The study of how living organisms function and interact with their environment.

- _First Aid_: The provision of immediate medical care in emergencies or injuries.


**V. Arts and Humanities:**

- _Art_: The study and practice of visual or performing arts, such as painting, sculpture, music, or theater.

- _Music_: The study of musical theory, composition, and performance.

- _Philosophy_: The study of fundamental questions about existence, knowledge, and ethics.

- _Religion_: The study of beliefs, practices, and moral values related to faith and spirituality.

- _Ethics_: The study of moral principles and values.


**VI. Business and Economics:**

- _Business Studies_: The study of business principles, management, and entrepreneurship.

- _Economics_: The study of the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services.

- _Accounting_: The practice of recording, analyzing, and reporting financial transactions.

- _Marketing_: The activities involved in promoting and selling products or services.

- _Finance_: The management of money and investments.


**VII. Computer Science and Technology:**

- _Computer Programming_: The process of writing instructions for computers to execute.

- _Information Technology (IT)_: The use and management of computer systems, networks, and data.

- _Web Design_: The creation and maintenance of websites and web applications.

- _Software Engineering_: The development and maintenance of software programs.

- _Artificial Intelligence (AI)_: The study and development of computer systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence.
