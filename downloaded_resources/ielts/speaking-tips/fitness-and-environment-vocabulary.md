In the left column are the words that are quite common or the candidate uses incorrect semantics. In the right column are the words you should use when taking the IELTS test to get a high score.

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quite common** | **Should use** | **Example** |
| health | fitness | Swimming is good for my physical and mental fitness. |
| do actions | do exercise | We need to do some exercise after a hard-working day. |
| be at home | stuck at home | I used to be stuck at home all day long. |
| rest | do/promote relaxation | You can do/promote relaxation by walking along the beach. |
| refreshed | revitalised | After walking around in such a peaceful place, I feel revitalised. |
| good at sport | athletic | Sam is very athletic at track and field. |
| endurance | stamina | Long-distance race training helps foster my stamina. |
