Boost your confidence & Speaking score with expert advice, feedback, and guided practice from an experienced IELTS trainer.

## Key content

By the end of this webinar, you'll be able to:

- plan your short talk using two planning strategies
- organize and develop your answer using a simple structure
- use a range of language related to holidays & travel
